Photo converter for the Kobo frame

Everything happens in your browser: photos are never uploaded anywhere. Output is 600×800 grayscale PNG, ready for E:\.frame\photos\.

Drop your photos here or click to choose them
JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC if your browser can open it…
E-ink screens are dark and low-contrast: a bit more contrast and a gamma above 1.0 usually improves photos. Changes show instantly in the thumbnails.
Files are numbered 001.png, 002.png… in list order (use ▲ ▼ to reorder).
How to use them
  1. Download the ZIP and unzip it.
  2. Delete the three sample photos from E:\.frame\photos\ and copy the new ones.
  3. If you made them in landscape, the Kobo screen will still show them rotated: choose that option only if the frame will lie on its side (you can also use “Rotate photo”).
